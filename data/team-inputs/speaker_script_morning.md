# Speaker script — morning check-in (Hajin Song, YUCN; from team Google Doc, Yonsei section)

Scenario: 8:00 AM — the AI speaker reminds Mrs. Kim to take her morning medication and checks how she is feeling.

| Speaker | Korean | English |
|---|---|---|
| AI | 할머니, 좋은 아침이에요! 아침 약 드실 시간이에요. | Good morning, Mrs. Kim! It's time for your morning medicine. |
| AI | 약 드시고 나면 저한테 말씀해 주세요. | Please tell me when you've taken it. |
| Patient | 응, 방금 물이랑 같이 먹었어. | Yes, I just took it with some water. |
| AI | 네, 아침 약 드신 걸로 적어둘게요. | Okay, I'll mark your morning medicine as taken. |
| AI | 오늘은 몸이 좀 어떠세요? 불편한 곳은 없으세요? | How are you feeling today? Is anything bothering you? |
| Patient | 아까부터 일어나면 좀 어지럽네. | I feel a little dizzy when I stand up. |
| AI | 그러셨군요. 어지러운 건 약 드시기 전부터 그랬나요? | I see. Did you feel dizzy before taking your medicine? |
| Patient | 응, 약 먹기 전부터 그랬어. | Yes, I felt dizzy before taking it. |
| AI | 알겠어요. 적어둘게요. 우선 앉아서 쉬시고, 어지러움이 계속되면 병원에 연락해 보세요. | Got it. I'll write that down. Please sit and rest for now, and call your doctor if the dizziness continues. |
| AI | (5분 뒤) 지금은 어떠신가요? 아직도 어지러우세요? | (5 min later) How are you now? Still dizzy? |

Maps to assessment list: A01 taken → A04 new symptom → S07 dizziness → A05 onset before/after dose → T03/T02 guidance + 5-min re-check.
